# Knight Family Organiser

Knight Family Organiser is a private tool one family uses to manage its own shared Google Calendar. It adds things like school term dates and regular activities to the family's calendar so everyone can see the week ahead.

It isn't a public service. There's nothing to sign up for, and it's only ever used with the owner's own Google account.
